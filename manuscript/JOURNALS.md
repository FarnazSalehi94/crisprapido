# Journal fit assessment — editorial planning, not acceptance prediction

Reviewed **2026-11-11**. Official scope/author-guideline pages below were identified through web search. This assessment concerns scientific fit and evidence gaps; **current exact article categories, limits, fees, display counts and submission policies must be directly rechecked before submission**. No acceptance rate, impact factor or publisher rule is invented from search titles/snippets.

## Bioinformatics

**Potential fit:** a computational methods/software contribution with a clear algorithmic advance, rigorous comparative validation and useful biological/computational application. A PAM-constrained indexed traversal and explicit separation of discovery/eligible alignment/scoring could form that story **if its novelty and supported scope are demonstrated against prior methods**.

**Current weaknesses:** only designed small new controls, no source/profile-equivalent Sassy comparison, historical comparator/PAM differences, no general completeness proof or broad held-out/biological evaluation. Native integration alone is not evidence of novel algorithmic contribution. Historical large-input timings cannot be presented as current matched five-mode results.

**Recommendation:** consider after verified literature positioning, pseudocode/invariant review, stronger independent bounded/held-out/external-tool comparison, and a prespecified application. A six-topic figure plan likely needs consolidation or a full-methods format; do not assume a short software format can contain it. Article-format decision is TODO.

## NAR Genomics and Bioinformatics

**Potential fit:** genome-analysis methodology with a reproducible software/data story and a convincing assembly/haplotype application. The separation of sequence/PAM recovery, coordinate projection, variant-associated candidate changes and honest scoring availability could make a coherent genomics article.

**Current weaknesses:** HG00735_pat is one haplotype with one-/three-guide workflow audits, not a population-level pangenome study or biological off-target validation. Variant-specific gains/losses require projection/sequence evidence, not raw assembly-coordinate set differences. Reliable research installation/model access and complete code/data provenance still matter.

**Recommendation:** a strong candidate **if** the paper develops the genomics application with prespecified sampling and independently validated interpretation. Without that extension, pitch it as a narrowly scoped computational research method rather than a comprehensive HPRC analysis. Additional large experiments require a separate plan/approval.

## Bioinformatics Advances

**Potential fit:** a research-software/methodology article emphasizing practical workflow improvements, reproducibility, transparent limitations and useful validation. The current architecture/concordance/reproducibility story is a natural presentation candidate once the comparisons are scientifically fair and software access is usable.

**Current weaknesses:** the same source/exporter comparability and installation/model-rights issues remain; changing venue does not justify unsupported recall, performance or biological claims. A pretrained scorer integration is not automatically model-development novelty.

**Recommendation:** a pragmatic venue to consider for a carefully bounded, reproducible software contribution, with scoring supplementary and a modest assembly demonstration if no large pangenome claim is made. This is a topical-fit assessment, **not** a claim of lower standards or easier acceptance. Verify the current article formats directly.

## Cross-journal submission gate

The recommended evidence package is our advice, not a quotation of publisher requirements:

1. Stable versioned code/archived revision and complete runnable research environment; explicitly optional pretrained-model setup with provenance/licensing reviewed.
2. A comparison matrix of supported semantics/profiles, exact input/output definitions, source-attested comparators, and complete discordance ledgers.
3. Independent eligibility/oracle checks, separate site/alignment/score results, and correctly scoped performance/memory/replicate claims.
4. Honest application scope: no unperformed cohort, fabricated variant discovery or biological score calibration.
5. Verified citations, author contributions, funding/conflicts, data/code accessions and current journal instructions. **TODO before submission.**

**Provisional positioning:** choose between a genomics-application-led NAR GAB story and a software/reproducibility-led Bioinformatics Advances story after filling the scope-specific gaps. Bioinformatics remains plausible only with a convincingly positioned and tested methods contribution. None is submission-ready on an unqualified five-mode/full-pangenome claim today.
